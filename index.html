What’s inside

One app, four field tools

Field guide

Every species has a photo, its common and scientific name, ID notes, habitat, distribution, IUCN Red List status, and whether it is venomous or poisonous. Lists are filtered to your state’s checklist.

Identify

Answer a few quick questions (group, colours, pattern, time of day) and get the most likely species where you are. Works offline once a pack is downloaded.

Checklists

Record sightings eBird-style. Choose a protocol and the app times the checklist, tracks GPS distance, and takes counts, life stage, photos and notes for each species. Data syncs when you’re back online.

Road-kill logger

Drive or walk a road while the app records the track. Drop a point at every carcass. Survey effort is kept, so results come out as kills per 100 km.

Safety first, always free

Venomous and poisonous flags and snakebite first-aid are never behind a paywall.

Sensitive species protected

Public maps blur locations to about 1 km, or about 10 km for traded and threatened species. Exact points are seen only by the observer and the reviewers.
