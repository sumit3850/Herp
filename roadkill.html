Road-kill Logger

A dedicated app for road-survey teams. It records your GPS track (survey effort), drops a point at every carcass, and reports kills per 100 km. It keeps working with no signal and uploads when you’re back online.

1

Install it on the phone used on surveys (no app store needed).

2

Sign in once with your HerpWatch account while you have internet. It’s the same account as the field app.

3

Open it from the home-screen icon on every survey. Have a passenger log the records, never the driver.
